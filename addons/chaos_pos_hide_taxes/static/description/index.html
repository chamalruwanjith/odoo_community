POS Hide Taxes on Receipt

Simplify Your POS Receipts

Overview

This module allows you to hide tax breakdown information from POS receipts, providing a cleaner and simpler receipt format for your customers.

Key Features

Easy Configuration

Simple checkbox in POS Settings to enable or disable tax display. No coding required!

Per-POS Control

Configure each Point of Sale independently. Different stores can have different settings.

Multi-Company Ready

Perfect for multi-company setups. Each company can configure its POS receipts independently.

What Gets Hidden?

When Tax Hiding is Enabled:

  • Tax breakdown section
  • Untaxed amounts (subtotals)
  • Individual tax group details

What Remains Visible:

  • Order lines with prices
  • Total amount (taxes included)
  • Payment details
  • Change amount
  • All other receipt elements

Configuration

Simple 3-Step Setup:

  1. Navigate to Point of Sale → Configuration → Settings
  2. Select your Point of Sale and enable "Hide Taxes on Receipt" in the Bills & Receipts section
  3. Save and restart your POS session

Use Cases

Retail Stores

Provide cleaner receipts to customers who don't need detailed tax information.

Restaurants & Cafes

Simplify receipts for quick-service environments where speed matters.

International Business

Different countries have different requirements - configure per location.
